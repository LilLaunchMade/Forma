# Community code of conduct

Treat every contributor and user with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Give specific, constructive feedback and assume good intent while remaining accountable for harm.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct and persistent unwanted contact are not acceptable. Keep technical discussions focused on the work and respect others' boundaries and privacy.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions, restrict participation, or ban repeat or serious offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent. Reports can be sent privately to repository maintainers using GitHub's available private contact or moderation channels; do not publish personal information in an issue. Maintainers should respond confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
